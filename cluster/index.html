Cluster Triage Simulator

You're the night-shift support engineer for a 256-GPU H100 cluster: 32 HGX nodes, NVLink inside each node, eight 400G InfiniBand rails between them, Slurm on top. Customers are training models on it right now.

Over an 8-hour shift (8 minutes at 1×) a chaos monkey injects real-world faults at random: GPUs falling off the bus, ECC row remaps, NVLink and InfiniBand failures, a Fabric Manager version mismatch, fans, power supplies, full disks, and the classic "it's not the hardware, it's your code."

  • Diagnose with the commands you'd really run.
  • Act: drain, reset, power-cycle, dispatch a tech, open an RMA, or tell the customer it's their code.
  • Verify and return the node to service. Every idle GPU-hour costs money.

Training mode shows hints. On-call mode doesn't. The randomness comes from a chaos source that will switch to a quantum random number generator when it's online.
