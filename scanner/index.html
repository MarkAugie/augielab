Drive the scanner

A mobile laser scanner paints a road with millions of points as it drives. But its idea of where it is slowly drifts, so the scanned road bends and sags. Survey control nails pin it back to the real world. Set the control, plant a blunder, and see whether your checkpoints catch it.
